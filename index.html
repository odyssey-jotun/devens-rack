Good vintage is hard to find

Secondhand clothing grew 14% in 2024 (ThredUp, 2025 Resale Report). The good pieces are still buried.

  • The racks are a mess

    Thrift stores cram thousands of unsorted hangers together. One pair of real bootcut Levi's can take weeks of trips to turn up.

  • Buying online feels like a gamble

    Blurry photos, missing measurements and vague sizes leave shoppers guessing. A piece that arrives two sizes small feels like money thrown away.

  • Good clothes deserve a second life

    Americans sent 11.3 million tons of textiles to landfills in 2018, according to the EPA. Jeans with years of wear left belong on someone's legs.

Deven does the digging

Deven knows the hunt firsthand. He combs Little Rock thrift racks for early-2000s denim and graphic tees, checks every piece for flaws, and shoots it flat so buyers can see exactly what arrives. He's also teaching himself to alter clothes, so some pieces leave with a better fit than the thrift store gave them.
